## 7. Coursework and revision support (OISF Week)

The weeks before coursework deadlines are often particularly stressful, with students facing multiple missed deadlines. The meeting discussed running a dedicated support week to help students catch up before new coursework or lectures begin.

This could also allow UWCS to create revision material incrementally, which could later be used for revision lectures before exams. This would avoid asking academics to create large amounts of material during their own exam periods.

One suggestion was to run this during Week 6 of each term, before lectures finish, while students can still ask their lecturers for clarification.

Bharat suggested a lab-style format, with people available to answer questions in Comp Café. The boundary around helping with active coursework would need to be made clear, particularly where coursework has not yet been released.

Ali noted that the support should cover content-heavy modules as well as programming-heavy coursework.

Sean suggested modelling the sessions on department-run support sessions. Ali felt that UWCS-style revision sessions might work better, but noted that a previous session had low attendance. Focusing each session on a specific module could make the purpose and expected content clearer.
