# 💜 Welfare Notice
UWCS has two Welfare Officers: Kevin (Discord: yummycatfood) and Erica (Discord: heldesar). They can also be reached via their email inbox at welfare@uwcs.co.uk.

Heading off to uni, or coming back for another year, can feel overwhelming.
