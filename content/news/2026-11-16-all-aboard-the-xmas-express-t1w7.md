Good morning, ladies and gentemen, and welcome onboard this UWCS service to The Christmas Holidays. 

Calling at: CS130 Problem Set 3, CS275 Problem Set 4, CS263 Coursework, CS118 Coursework 2 and The Cristmas Holidays, where this service terminates.

First class accomodation is located towards the front of the train, and is reserved exclusively for passengers who have already completed their coursework. We expect this carriage to remain empty for the duration of our journey.

An onboard bar service is available in Coach C, serving a selection of alcoholic beverages and Fruit Shoots as well as a seasonal offering of rapidly diminishing academic motivation. 

Please make sure you have your submissions and AI declarations ready for inspection, as our train managers (~~exec~~) will be making our way through the train soon. Passengers travelling without found without a valid submission reciept will be subject to pay a 5% Tabula penalty fare.

And finally, if you see something that doesn't look right, speak to exec or email the welfare officers at `welfare@uwcs.co.uk`. We'll sort it.
