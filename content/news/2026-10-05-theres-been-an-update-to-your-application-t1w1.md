### 💜 Welfare Notice

UWCS has two Welfare Officers: Kevin (Discord: `yummycatfood`) and Erica (Discord: `heldesar`). They can also be reached via their email inbox at welfare@uwcs.co.uk.

Heading off to uni, or coming back for another year, can feel overwhelming. You can speak to Kevin and Erica at any time via Discord, email, or in person for advice, support, and help navigating the uni’s services, or to raise concerns about behaviour at our events. No problem is too small to reach out about.

Any discussions you have with them will be treated sensitively. They will not share details more widely without your permission, except where they believe there is a serious risk of harm, a safeguarding concern, or another situation where they are required to escalate.
