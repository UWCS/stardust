## Update on R+S

An Exec Member has submitted an R+S report (not society-related), which is one of the first to be handled by the new team. They joined the meeting to share with the welfare team how R+S has changed, so that they can keep this in mind when handling potential situations in the upcoming year.

The main change was that R+S created an investigation (stage two) based on the evidence they had provided, without their input. This is an action usually completed by the reporter. Raven later corroborated that this was likely. The meeting consensus was that this was neither a good nor bad thing.

The Exec Member feels removed from the situation, but is glad that something is coming out of the report. This was outlined not as a reason to avoid dealing with R+S, but as something for the welfare team to keep in mind. This isn't expected to be a typical occurrence due to the unusual nature of their report.
